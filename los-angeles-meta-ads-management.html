Before Launch

Why ads can get attention without producing customers

A campaign can reach thousands of people and still fail when the offer is vague, the audience is too broad, the creative does not communicate value, the landing page lacks trust or leads receive slow follow-up. We evaluate the full path from the first impression to the sales conversation.

Unclear offer

Prospects should immediately understand what is being offered, who it is for and what to do next.

Weak landing experience

The page or form should match the ad, answer the main objections and make the next step simple on a phone.

Poor lead qualification

Questions and targeting should reduce low-intent submissions without making the form unnecessarily difficult.

Slow follow-up

Fresh leads cool quickly. A clear response process matters as much as the ad that created the inquiry.

Meta advertising questions

What does Meta ads management include?

The scope can include offer and audience planning, ad messaging and creative direction, campaign setup, lead forms or landing-page recommendations, measurement and ongoing adjustments.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Advertising spend is paid directly to Meta and is separate from management fees.

Can Meta ads guarantee sales?

No. Results depend on the offer, audience, creative, budget, competition, sales process and follow-up. We do not promise guaranteed sales.
