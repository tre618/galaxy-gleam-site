Website Redesign Guide

Website redesign in Los Angeles: when should a small business rebuild?

Published August 3, 2026 · Galaxy Gleam Media

A redesign should solve a business problem, not merely replace colors. Start by identifying what prevents visitors from understanding the offer, trusting the company or contacting it.

Signs your website needs a redesign

  • Important pages are difficult to use on a phone.
  • Services, location or contact information are outdated.
  • Visitors cannot quickly identify the main offer or next step.
  • Pages load slowly because of oversized images or unnecessary scripts.
  • The site cannot support useful service pages, analytics or basic SEO.

Define the redesign scope before choosing a platform

List the pages that must remain, the content that needs rewriting, the forms or booking tools required, and who will maintain the site after launch. A clear scope keeps the project focused and makes quotes easier to compare.

Protect existing SEO during the move

Record current URLs before rebuilding. Keep valuable URLs when possible, redirect retired pages to the closest relevant replacement, preserve useful copy, add canonical tags, and submit an updated sitemap. Broken redirects and missing pages can waste the visibility an older site already earned.

Design around the buyer’s next action

Each service page should answer who the service is for, what is included, what the process looks like and how to contact the business. On mobile, calls to action should be visible without forcing visitors to hunt through menus.

What to prepare for a faster redesign

  • Approved logo, colors and business name
  • Accurate services and service areas
  • Current phone, email and booking links
  • Photos you have permission to publish
  • Any legal, privacy or industry-specific requirements
