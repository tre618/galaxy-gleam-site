Los Angeles Local SEO

A practical local SEO checklist for Los Angeles businesses.

Published July 30, 2026 · Galaxy Gleam Media

Local rankings are built from relevance, distance and prominence signals. You cannot control every factor, but you can make the business easier for customers and search engines to understand and trust.

Google Business Profile

  • Use the correct primary category and only relevant additional categories
  • Keep phone, website, hours and service area accurate
  • Add real photos and complete service descriptions
  • Respond professionally to genuine reviews

Website service and location signals

  • Create a useful page for each major service
  • Explain who the service helps, the process and common questions
  • Use Los Angeles naturally where it is genuinely relevant
  • Connect related pages with descriptive internal links

Business information and citations

Keep the business name, phone number and website consistent on reputable directories, chambers and partner pages. Avoid mass submissions to low-quality sites.

Reviews and proof

Ask real customers for honest reviews without telling them what rating to leave. Add accurate portfolio examples and case studies with permission. Never fabricate testimonials or outcomes.

Technical checks

  • HTTPS works without errors
  • Pages are mobile friendly and reasonably fast
  • Important URLs appear in the XML sitemap
  • Canonical tags point to the correct version
  • Search Console is monitored for indexing problems
Next step: Prioritize the service with the best combination of demand, profit and proof. Build authority around that topic before expanding everywhere.
